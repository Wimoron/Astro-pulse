**Full description** (max 4000):

Planets around other stars are far too faint to see. Astronomers find them by the clues they leave behind. AstroPulse teaches you those tricks, then lets you use them on real NASA data.

LEARN FIVE WAYS TO FIND A PLANET
• Transit: watch a star dim as a planet crosses it
• Wobble: see a star's colour shift as a planet tugs on it
• Direct photo: spot the planet itself in an image
• Flash: catch a brief extra brightening from gravity bending light
• Position: track the tiny circle a star traces on the sky
Each short lab explains every step in plain words and ends with a planet you measured yourself. Switch to Expert mode to see the numbers.

PLAY THE DAILY HUNT
A new puzzle every day: a star watched for 27 days. Is a planet hiding in it, or is it two stars eclipsing, or nothing at all? Find the repeat, make your call and get scored. Keep your streak going and practise on unlimited extra puzzles.

JUDGE REAL CANDIDATES
Look at real TESS telescope data for planet candidates that NASA has not confirmed yet. Check the warning signs, make your call, and see later whether NASA agreed.

EXPLORE THE SKY AND THE CATALOGUE
Tap stars on an interactive sky map. Browse thousands of known planet-hosting stars from the NASA Exoplanet Archive, search any star by name or TESS number, or load your own light-curve CSV.

EARN YOUR RANK
Collect planets, earn badges and climb from Cadet to Legend. Share a discovery card with friends.

FREE AND PRIVATE
No account. No ads. No tracking. Your progress stays on your phone.

Data: NASA Exoplanet Archive, MAST (TESS and Kepler), OGLE. AstroPulse is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.
